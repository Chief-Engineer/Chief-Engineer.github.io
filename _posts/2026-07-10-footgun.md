## Review and Responses
As part of an effort to avoid incorrect, misleading, or biased content, multiple sources familiar with events reviewed versions or segments of the timeline. In addition, some subjects were contacted for an opportunity to respond, including to correct incorrect or missing information. Some of those subjects are listed below.

### Wizard's Den
A representative for Wizard's Den was largely responsive to questions and requests related to this timeline. In the past, Wizard's Den has not consistently been truthful or forthcoming. They certainly have bias with regard to the events detailed here. However, their responses appear to have been largely consistent with what could be verified using other sources.

### PJB
[Multiple attempts](/assets/2026-06-credibility/2.png) were made to reach PJB, but she essentially refused to answer questions. PJB has made [conflicting statements]({% post_url 2026-06-25-credibility %}) about the 2024 admin departure. PJB [reportedly disputed](https://notaslug.org/posts/wizden-implosion/#commentsclarifications-from-pjb) the idea she took a break shortly after the Governance Internal Letter was sent, despite describing it as a break both [before](/assets/2026-07-footgun/break-26-5-3.png) and [after](/assets/2026-07-footgun/break-26-6-12.png) the publication of that post. For these and other similar reasons, I must largely rely on public statements from PJB for her perspective of events, and her statements cannot necessarily be taken at face value.
